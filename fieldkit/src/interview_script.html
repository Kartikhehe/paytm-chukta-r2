L2
Apne 2–3 supplier jod dein aur raat 9 baje ka reminder on kar dein?
They add suppliers themselves (Suppliers → Supplier jodein) and switch on the 9 pm toggle. Yes only if both happen.
L2 Supplier + reminder on
L3
Jo bill aaj ya kal dena hi hai, kya woh yahin se Paytm se chukta karenge?
Capture that bill → Abhi chukta opens a UPI intent; owner picks Paytm and enters their own PIN. If Paytm refuses (NPCI blocks link payments to personal UPI IDs and small non-verified merchants): owner opens Paytm, scans the supplier's printed QR, types the amount and writes INV-<no> in the note. Then tap "Paid via Paytm (owner confirmed)".
L3 Paid real bill via Paytm: Yes / No / Already paid / No bill due
L4
90-din ke pilot mein shaamil honge?
Take area + shop type only. Contact details go on a separate paper, never in the workbook.
L4 Pilot sign-up
